What is an early signal?

At least $5K liquidity and $1K 24-hour volume. These are activity filters, not a prediction or a safety check.

Why might data be missing?

New launches may not yet have market data. Recent pools are not necessarily new tokens, and the live feed does not cover every Solana token.
