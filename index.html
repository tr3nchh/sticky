What is an early signal?

At least $5K liquidity and $1K 24-hour volume on a matching DexScreener pair. These are activity filters, not a prediction or a safety check.

Why might data be missing?

Market cap can take time to appear. When unavailable, FDV is shown separately and may differ from market cap. SOL launch estimates are snapshots, not live values. Recent pools are not necessarily newly created tokens.
